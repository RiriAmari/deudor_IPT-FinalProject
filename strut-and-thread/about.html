About Strut & Thread

A small Manila label making clothes that feel good, last long and look like you.

Clothes with a little confidence

Strut & Thread started in 2019 on a single rolling rack in a Makati studio. We wanted everyday clothes that suit Philippine weather and city life: breathable in the heat, warm enough for the office air-conditioning, and sharp enough for dinner afterwards.

The name says it all. Strut is how you feel when something fits right. Thread is where it starts: good yarn, honest fabric and careful stitching.

Today we design for women, men and kids, working with small mills and workshops across Luzon and the Visayas. We make in short runs, so we make what people will wear.

What we care about

Natural fabrics

Linen, cotton, merino and leather make up most of what we sell. They breathe, age well and feel better on the skin.

Fair making

We pay our partner workshops on time and visit them twice a year. Every maker is named in our product notes.

Less, but better

Fewer styles, made in smaller batches. That means less waste and pieces you will reach for again.

Fits real people

Our sizes are tested on a fit panel of 40 people across a range of heights and shapes.

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Metro Manila orders arrive in 1–3 business days. Other areas in the Philippines take 3–7 business days. International orders take 7–14 days.

What is your return policy?

Return any full-price item within 30 days, unworn and with tags, for a full refund. Sale items can be returned within 14 days.

How do I find my size?

Each category page has a sizing guide, and every product shows the sizes available. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit.

Which payment methods do you accept?

GCash, Maya, major credit and debit cards, and cash on delivery within Metro Manila.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Message us within two hours of ordering and we will update or cancel it before it is packed.
