From linen dresses for humid afternoons to chunky cardigans for air-conditioned offices. Easy pieces that mix and match.

Sizing guide

XS fits a 32″ bust and 25″ waist. Each size adds about 2″. Between sizes? Size up for a relaxed fit.

Fabric care

Wash knits cold and dry flat. Linen softens with every wash and is best air-dried.
